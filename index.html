    The Sheetz breakfast menu, explained

    Sheetz is a convenience store chain with a full kitchen, and breakfast is one of the things it does best. You order on a touchscreen, customise almost everything, and at most stores you can get breakfast any time of day or night.

    Breakfast at Sheetz is served 24/7

    Unlike most fast-food chains, the Sheetz breakfast menu isn't limited to the morning. Most stores serve breakfast 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. That makes Sheetz a favourite for early commutes, night shifts and road trips. A few stores, such as some in travel plazas or special venues, may keep different hours. Store and holiday times are listed on the Sheetz Hours page.

    What the "Sh" names mean

    Sheetz breakfast sandwiches are named after their bread, with a playful "Sh" twist:

    • ShmuffinEnglish muffin · $3.99
    • Shmiscuitbiscuit · $3.99
    • Shmagelbagel · $4.59 (Everything Shmagel too)
    • Croissant Sandwichcroissant · $3.99
    • Sourdough, Flatbread & Waffle$4.59 each
    • Breakfast Meltzgrilled melt · $4.39

    You build each one on the touchscreen, choosing your egg, meat, cheese and extras. Bread on its own, from a pretzel roll to a waffle, costs about $1.59.

    Burritos, tacos and the 2/$6 deal

    The cheapest hot breakfast on the Sheetz breakfast menu is a $3.19 burrito: Bacon, Egg & Cheese, Sausage, Egg & Cheese, or Western. For two people, the 2/$6 Classic Breakfast Burritos deal is the best value, saving 38¢ over two singles. Hungrier? The Grande Burrito is about $6.39, or $6.99 as a Grande Burrito Bowl without the tortilla. Breakfast Tacos are about $4.99 and the Breakfast Quesadilla about $4.59.

    Subs, platter and Eat Shmart

    Made-to-order breakfast subs on a honey wheat, pretzel, white or flatbread roll are about $4.99. The Classic Breakfast Platter is the biggest sit-down-style plate at about $6.69. For lighter or low-carb mornings, the Eat Shmart range includes the Farmhouse and Wildwest Flatbreads ($4.99 and $4.39), the Protein Showdown ($4.99) and three Keto Cups (Farmhouse and Mexican $4.19, Sausage $3.69).

    Sidez and Shweetz

    Round out breakfast with Mini Hashbrowns ($2.99), French Toast Sticks ($3.89), Cinnapocketz ($4.89) or Cheesy Bacon Tater Bombs ($4.89).

    Coffee with breakfast

    Most people pair the Sheetz breakfast menu with a coffee from the self-serve bar or a made-to-order espresso drink from the touchscreen. Sizes, flavours and prices for hot coffee, lattes, frozen drinks and cold brew are on the Sheetz Coffee Menu page.

    How to order breakfast at Sheetz

    1. Use the touchscreen or the app. Choose a category, then your bread, meat, cheese and extras.
    2. Customise freely. Add or swap ingredients. Some extras may cost a little more.
    3. Pay and pick up. Your order number is called when the food is ready.
    4. Earn rewards. Sheetz Rewardz members earn points on purchases.

    Cheapest Sheetz breakfasts

    On a tight budget, the cheapest options are a $3.19 burrito, the Sausage Keto Cup ($3.69), a Shmuffin, Shmiscuit or Croissant Sandwich ($3.99) and the 2/$6 burrito deal for two. Add Mini Hashbrowns for $2.99 and breakfast still comes in well under $10.

    Best items on the Sheetz breakfast menu

    If it's your first time, these are the Sheetz breakfast menu items people talk about most:

    • Shmuffin – the classic English muffin breakfast sandwich, about $3.99.
    • Bacon, Egg & Cheese Burrito – quick, cheap and easy to eat in the car at $3.19.
    • Grande Burrito – the most filling burrito on the Sheetz breakfast menu at about $6.39.
    • Breakfast Meltz – a grilled, cheesy melt for about $4.39.
    • Mini Hashbrowns – the side almost everyone adds, about $2.99.
    • Cinnapocketz – a sweet treat to share, about $4.89.

    Sheetz breakfast menu for road trips

    Sheetz stores sit along highways across Pennsylvania, Ohio, Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina and Michigan, which makes the Sheetz breakfast menu a road-trip staple. Burritos, Shmuffins and keto cups are easy to eat on the move, and the 24/7 breakfast means it doesn't matter if you leave at 4 a.m. or roll in after midnight. For a car full of people, use the order calculator above, then grab two Classic Breakfast Burritos for $6 per pair to keep the bill low.

    Sheetz breakfast menu for families and groups

    Feeding a family from the Sheetz breakfast menu is cheap compared with a sit-down breakfast. Four $3.19 burritos and two Mini Hashbrowns come to about $18.74 before tax. With the 2/$6 deal on Classic Breakfast Burritos, four burritos and two hashbrowns drop to about $17.98. Picky eaters can each customise their own sandwich on the touchscreen, so everyone gets exactly what they want.

    Allergens and nutrition

    Because almost every item on the Sheetz breakfast menu is made to order, ingredients change with your choices. Sheetz shows nutrition details in its app and on the touchscreen. If you have an allergy, check the ingredients for your exact build and talk to the team before ordering.

    About these prices

    Prices on this page are typical prices from our last check in November 2026. They can vary by store, by state and with your choice of add-ons, and sales tax depends on where you buy. Check the touchscreen or app for the exact price at your Sheetz.

    Sheetz breakfast FAQ

    Does Sheetz serve breakfast all day?

    Yes. Most Sheetz stores serve breakfast 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. A few locations may have different hours.

    How much is a Sheetz breakfast burrito?

    Single breakfast burritos (Bacon, Egg & Cheese, Sausage, Egg & Cheese or Western) cost about $3.19. Two Classic Breakfast Burritos are $6 with the 2/$6 deal, and the Grande Burrito is about $6.39.

    What is a Shmuffin at Sheetz?

    A Shmuffin is a Sheetz breakfast sandwich on an English muffin, about $3.99. A Shmiscuit is on a biscuit and a Shmagel is on a bagel.

    What is the cheapest breakfast at Sheetz?

    A single bread is about $1.59, and the cheapest hot breakfast is a $3.19 burrito. The Sausage Keto Cup is about $3.69 and a Shmuffin or Shmiscuit about $3.99.

    Does Sheetz have low-carb breakfast options?

    Yes. The Eat Shmart range includes Keto Cups (Farmhouse, Mexican and Sausage, about $3.69 to $4.19), the Protein Showdown and two flatbreads.

    What sides can I get with Sheetz breakfast?

    Popular sides include Mini Hashbrowns (about $2.99), French Toast Sticks ($3.89), Cinnapocketz ($4.89) and Cheesy Bacon Tater Bombs ($4.89).

    How do I order breakfast at Sheetz?

    Order on the in-store touchscreen or in the Sheetz app. Choose a breakfast category, then customise your bread, egg, meat, cheese and extras.
